What the numbers mean

↓ Download speed

How quickly data reaches your device. Higher download speed helps with streaming, large downloads and loading media-heavy pages.

↑ Upload speed

How quickly your device can send data. Upload matters for video calls, cloud backups and sending large files.

↔ Ping / latency

The round-trip response time to the Cloudflare edge. Lower latency generally feels more responsive for gaming, calls and interactive apps.

Why results can vary

Wi-Fi signal, VPNs, device load, browser limits and nearby network traffic can all change the result. Treat this as a quick estimate, not a certified ISP benchmark.
